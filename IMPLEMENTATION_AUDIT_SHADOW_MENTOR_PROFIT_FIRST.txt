IMPLEMENTED CHANGE
- Replaced the circular global Mentor/Calibration lock with PER-EDGE prospective Shadow Mentor trials.
- A newly PROVEN profit EDGE may compute a counterfactual shadow confidence, but live/applied confidence remains unchanged until that EDGE proves its own calibration contribution.
- Each EDGE needs at least 100 future matched cases.
- Eligibility requires Shadow Brier < comparison Brier and a positive one-sided lower confidence bound on paired Brier gain (z=1.645).
- Trials are MARGINAL per EDGE:
  * if other EDGE rules are already LIVE, the candidate EDGE is tested on top of the already-live Mentor contribution;
  * it cannot claim calibration improvement created by another EDGE;
  * global Mentor delta remains capped at +8%.
- New EDGE rules cannot inherit old/global Calibration evidence.
- SHIFT quarantines both live and shadow Mentor influence; WATCH attenuates it.
- Eligible status is recomputed from accumulated future evidence and can be revoked if later evidence no longer satisfies the gate.
